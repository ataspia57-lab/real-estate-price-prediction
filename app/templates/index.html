    How it works

    1. Data ingestion. Historical listings with area, size, bedrooms, bathrooms and price.
    2. Cleaning. Missing values filled with medians, impossible rows dropped, price outliers removed with the IQR rule, and rare areas grouped as “Other”.
    3. Model comparison. Linear Regression, Lasso, Decision Tree, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting are tuned and tested; the most accurate one is deployed.
    4. Prediction. Your inputs are one-hot encoded and passed to the saved model through a Flask API.
